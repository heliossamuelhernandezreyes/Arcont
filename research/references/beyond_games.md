# Cross-Disciplinary Knowledge Map

Arcont's Reference Lab intentionally stores adjacent knowledge. Relevance may emerge later.

## Human factors
- Motor control, reaction time and Fitts's law for touch/aim UI.
- Hick's law and choice overload for dense mobile controls.
- Attention, visual search and target salience.
- Cognitive load during multitasking.
- Motion sickness/vestibular conflict and camera comfort.
- Accessibility: motor, visual, auditory and cognitive accommodations.

## Perception and visual design
- Gestalt grouping.
- Contrast/color science and color-vision deficiency.
- Atmospheric perspective and depth cues.
- Silhouette recognition.
- Temporal readability of VFX.
- Small-screen typography and information hierarchy.

## Audio/perception
- Psychoacoustics, masking and localization.
- Transient perception and perceived impact.
- Dynamic range on phone speakers vs headphones.
- Auditory attention and threat cues.

## Robotics/control theory
Useful analogies for R-3 and AI systems: state estimation, noisy observations, feedback loops, bounded autonomy, task allocation and path planning.

## Architecture/software engineering
- Data-oriented design and cache behavior.
- Event-driven architecture.
- State machines and formal transition modeling.
- Observability/profiling.
- Property-based testing.
- Reproducible builds.
- Schema/version migration.

## Mathematics/geometry
- Vector/quaternion interpolation.
- Ballistics and numerical integration.
- Spatial partitioning.
- Graph search and heuristics.
- Probability distributions for spread/uncertainty.
- Signal filtering for camera/input smoothing.

## Film/animation
- Staging, anticipation, follow-through, timing, arcs and silhouette.
- Camera composition and lens/FOV language.
- Editing rhythm as inspiration for encounter pacing.

## Architecture/urbanism
- Sightlines, choke points, wayfinding, landmarks, defensible space and circulation can inform level design.

## Biology/anatomy
- Locomotion constraints and body mechanics can inform readable injury states and creature animation without requiring medical simulation.

## Operations/research discipline
- Keep hypotheses falsifiable.
- Separate measurement from intuition.
- Preserve negative results.
- Track provenance.
- Prefer small experiments before architecture-wide adoption.

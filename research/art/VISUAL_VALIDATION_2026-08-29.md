# Arcont — Visual validation 2026-08-29

Status: EVIDENCE / reference-lab

## Source
Gameplay screenshots generated by Godot CI #447 from `feat/cc0-provisional-art-pass` after the modular village pass was mounted and compiled.

## What the screenshots prove
- The modular architecture pass is actually present in runtime; the previous visual ambiguity is resolved.
- The houses now have a more differentiated silhouette than the legacy single-mesh buildings, but they are not visually acceptable yet.
- The primitive roof planes read as thin floating slabs from gameplay distance, especially on gable and hip variants.
- Several facades read as broad unbroken boxes; windows/doors do not create enough believable depth from the player camera.
- Building masses are too clean and toy-like for Arcont's target. There is insufficient material variation, trim, roof thickness, eaves, wall base, porch logic, damage and vegetation integration.
- The current village still reads primarily as an exposed gray/brown open space with structures around it rather than a believable settlement swallowed by forest.
- The terrain-following route implementation is visually intrusive: rectangular segmented strips are clearly visible in the north lookback and other views. Numeric terrain adherence is correct, but the representation is artistically rejected.
- Vegetation remains cyan/low-detail in the CI renderer and does not create a convincing forest wall. The current `tree_blocks.fbx` remains a technical placeholder, not target art.
- Lighting/atmosphere are still flat in CI captures. Do not use these captures to judge Android PBR fidelity or real-device performance, but do use them to judge silhouette, composition, spacing, intersections and obvious geometry defects.

## Acceptance rule
A visual pass is not accepted because CI is green or because metrics are correct. Acceptance requires screenshot/device inspection of:
1. silhouette and proportion;
2. grounding/intersections;
3. material read;
4. composition and landmark hierarchy;
5. route/cover readability;
6. forest/urban transition;
7. mobile performance on real Android before final budgets.

## Immediate corrections
1. Replace primitive roof-slab construction with thicker roof assemblies including eaves/fascia/gable closure and less exaggerated overhang.
2. Rebuild front facades from actual bays around door/window openings; avoid a centered split that ignores the selected door offset.
3. Add plinth/base course, lintels, corner trim, porch depth and secondary masses so houses read as assembled architecture rather than colored boxes.
4. Replace the 169 segmented BoxMesh route plates with purpose-built terrain-sampled continuous meshes or substantially larger coherent strips whose seams are not visible; preserve terrain ownership and visual-only collision contract.
5. Increase forest enclosure around village edges while preserving combat lanes and sightline control.

## Decision
ART-PASS-9 is technically integrated but visually **REJECTED / ITERATE**. The architecture system remains the correct production direction; the current primitive implementation is a greybox-quality intermediate, not a finished art pass.

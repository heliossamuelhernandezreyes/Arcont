# Asset Vault Collections

ARCONT clasifica recursos por utilidad práctica, no solamente por proveedor.

## 2D
- pixel-art: characters, enemies, tiles, props, VFX, UI.
- vector/HD: icons, HUD, menus, cards, maps, backgrounds.
- tilemaps: terrain, dungeons, cities, interiors.
- VFX: particles, explosions, smoke, magic, weather, impacts.

## 2.5D
- isometric kits.
- billboard/sprite-in-3D resources.
- 2D characters with 3D environments.
- orthographic modular environments.

## 3D
- mobile-lowpoly.
- stylized.
- realistic/PBR.
- characters and creatures.
- humanoid rigs and animation libraries.
- nature/vegetation/rocks/terrain.
- architecture/interiors/modular buildings.
- props/furniture/tools.
- vehicles.
- sci-fi/fantasy/modern/horror thematic kits.

## Materials and lighting
- seamless PBR materials.
- trim sheets/atlases.
- decals.
- HDRIs by indoor/outdoor/time/weather.

## Audio
- UI.
- impacts/destruction.
- footsteps/cloth/Foley.
- weapons where appropriate for fictional media production.
- vehicles/machinery.
- creatures/voices without identity-sensitive reuse.
- nature/weather/room tones/cities.
- music only when project-use and redistribution obligations are clear.

## Fonts/UI
- OFL families suitable for HUD, body text, display, fantasy, sci-fi and pixel aesthetics.
- icon sets and controller glyphs with explicit licensing.

## Priority filters

Every collection should be searchable by:
- license friction: CC0 > permissive attribution > custom project-use > restrictive.
- commercial use.
- attribution.
- redistribution.
- style/theme.
- 2D/2.5D/3D.
- engine compatibility.
- Android/mobile suitability.
- polycount/texture resolution/audio footprint.
- rig/animation availability.

## Initial high-value candidates

### Kenney
Seed broad 2D/UI/prototype collections first. Kenney's asset pages identify packs such as UI Pack and Development Essentials as CC0, making them excellent low-friction baseline resources.

### Poly Haven
Seed HDRI, textures/PBR and 3D models. Treat asset files as CC0 but do not assume site logos, example renders or user-submitted renders share that status.

### Quaternius
Seed metadata for mobile/stylized characters, environments and modular kits, including Universal Base Characters, Sci-Fi Essentials, Platformer, Dungeon and other kits. Do not mirror current QAL-governed asset files because standalone redistribution is restricted. Preserve exact asset-page/license evidence because older/current pages can differ in licensing presentation.

### OpenGameArt
Use primarily as a discovery pool. Each item receives its own license review before being recommended automatically.

### Freesound
Prefer CC0 sounds for the default frictionless pool. CC-BY goes into an attribution-required pool; CC-BY-NC is excluded from commercial-default recommendations.

### Sonniss GameAudioGDC
Treat as a large external production-use audio library, not as redistributable ARCONT content. Catalog categories and source bundles while users obtain files from the official source under the applicable license.

### Google Fonts / OFL
Create typography collections by role and preserve license notices with any redistributed font files.

## Definition of "absorbed"

For ARCONT, a source is absorbed when:
1. it is registered;
2. its licensing model is understood;
3. useful categories are indexed;
4. metadata can be queried consistently;
5. legal mirroring policy is known;
6. high-value assets/packs can be progressively enumerated without redesigning the catalog.

This is intentionally different from downloading every binary. A terabyte of unindexed files is less useful than a searchable, legally traceable vault.
